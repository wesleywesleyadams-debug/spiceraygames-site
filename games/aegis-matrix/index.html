Aegis Matrix™

Lead one of several distinct factions in real-time battles for star systems. Each faction plays differently, with its own technologies and strengths. Expand across planets, mine resources, research technology, and fight fleets that adapt to how you play.

Distinct factions

The Trade Federation, the Hive Mind and the Eldar Covenant, each with its own tech tree and bonuses.

Deep tech

A tech grid of Basic, Advanced and Experimental upgrades that change how your ships fight.

Real fleet battles

Lasers, rail cannons, drones, missiles and tractor beams, with every shot simulated.

Challenging AI

Face two kinds of opponent: AI and NAI, alone or in teams.
