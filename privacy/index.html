Aegis Matrix — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Spiceray Games LLC ("we", "us") makes Aegis Matrix ("the Game"). This policy explains what information the Game and our website handle. The short version: we do not run servers that collect your data, and the Game does not send us analytics, tracking or crash reports.

1. Information stored on your computer

The Game saves these files only on your own computer, in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Aegis Matrix on Windows:

  • settings and key bindings;
  • saved games, custom maps and match replays;
  • in-game profiles: the name you choose, and for password-protected profiles a one-way hash of the password (never the password itself);
  • for a Steam-linked profile, your Steam ID and Steam display name;
  • multiplayer error reports, if a match goes out of sync.

None of this is sent to us. You can delete it at any time by deleting that folder or uninstalling the Game.

2. Steam

If you play through Steam, the Game uses the Steamworks service provided by Valve Corporation to sign you in, host and join lobbies, and connect you to other players. Through Steam the Game reads your Steam ID and display name. Your display name is shown to other players in lobbies and matches. Valve handles this data under its own privacy policy: https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/

3. Multiplayer

In multiplayer, game commands and your player name are exchanged with the other players in your match. If you connect directly by IP address instead of through Steam, your IP address is visible to the players you connect to, as with any direct connection. We do not receive or store this information.

4. Our website and email

Our website, spiceraygames.com, is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., which processes technical data such as visitors' IP addresses to deliver the site, under its own privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement Our website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking of its own. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply to you. We do not sell or share your information.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about players, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the EU, UK or another place that gives you privacy rights, or you have any question about this policy, contact us and we will help.

7. Changes

If the Game starts collecting new information, we will update this policy and the date above before that change ships.
